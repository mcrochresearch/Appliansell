# Rivals Lose Users on Bookkeeping, Not Biometrics

Outside workout and food logging, people mostly complain about mechanical failures and broken trust, not missing science. **Sleep apps let a nap overwrite the night, and their alarms fail to sound.** **Running platforms charge people to see their own history**: Strava paywalled Year in Sport in December 2025 and put its API behind a subscription in June 2026, and Garmin launched Connect+. **Habit, meditation and fasting apps punish a sick day and trap people in renewals.** **AI coaches invent numbers about the user** (Whoop) or try to optimise away a dog walk (Fitbit's Gemini coach). **Weight and meal-planning apps lose or strand years of data**, and Mealime shuts on **21 October 2026, twelve days from now, with no export**. Underserved users add their own list: cycle predictions that break for contraception, PCOS and perimenopause; no structured way to train around an injury; jargon that loses beginners; and fixed font sizes and small controls that shut out older and blind users. nütn's free, offline-first, all-in-one design already avoids the paywall and lock-in complaints, and its backend coach rejects any number that isn't in the user's data, which is the safeguard Whoop lacked. The code check also found **three defects in shipping code that rivals are criticised for**. First, onboarding promises that "Programs will avoid aggravating movements", but nothing passes injuries to the program builder. Second, the fasting timer shows "Deep Autophagy" and "Immune Reset" stages that cannot be measured. Third, HealthKit sleep is summed into one 18:00–12:00 window, with no handling for naps, day sleep or overlapping sources. The highest-value new builds after those are listed in the build order at the end: session-based sleep import, de-duplicated and editable imported cardio, a free yearly recap timed before Strava's December paywall, a user-set pause/sick/travel mode, per-category notification controls, Dynamic Type that actually scales, and a Mealime recipe-import flow. As in round 1, every complaint reached us secondhand, so read the priorities as directional.

## Every complaint here arrived secondhand, often through rivals

All six research notes report the same block: reddit.com, apps.apple.com review pages, Trustpilot, the Whoop Community, AppleVis, unstar.app and many review sites returned proxy denials or DNS failures. **Nearly every quote is therefore a search-engine excerpt of the cited page, not a page anyone read in full**, and Reddit sentiment appears only where journalists or competitors quoted it. The meal-planning, progress-photo, fasting and habit evidence leans heavily on **competitor blogs** (MealThinker, Pann, Swoodie, Plan to Eat, HabitBox, Nutrola, GainFrame), and several "user quotes" come from aggregators such as justuseapp, Kimola and mwm.ai, whose counts are low-confidence. Vendor pages (Whoop Locker, Rise, RingConn, Oura) are used for product facts, not as proof of demand. Peer-reviewed and survey sources are noted where they exist: AASM 2025, JMIR mHealth 2021, Nature and Science of Sleep 2023, and a 522,994-user adherence cohort. "Widespread" means repeated across independent source types, never a measured frequency. Where this report cites nütn code, the path is relative to `nutn-app/` on the `claude/nutn-build-next` branch, which already contains round 1's build-next work.

## Sleep users want their nights counted correctly before they are scored

The new sleep complaints are about bookkeeping. A Garmin forum thread is titled "(Fake) Daytime nap replaces good night sleep data and tanks scores for previous couple of days. No way to correct after the fact" ([Garmin Forums](https://forums.garmin.com/outdoor-recreation/outdoor-recreation/f/epix-2/323872/11-25-fake-daytime-nap-replaces-good-night-sleep-data-and-tanks-scores-for-previous-couple-of-days-no-way-to-correct-after-the-fact)). On a Fenix 7, a 2 h 15 min nap replaced the night and produced a **zero sleep score** ([Garmin Forums](https://forums.garmin.com/outdoor-recreation/outdoor-recreation/f/fenix-7-series/314681/night-sleep-data-gets-replaced-by-daytime-sleep/1527514)). Oura's nap rules are rigid: 15 minutes to 3 hours, and usually not within 4 hours of the main sleep ([Oura Support](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/articles/1500009653181)). AutoSleep logs watching TV as sleep ([Marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/apps/autosleep-watch-sleep-tracker)). Whoop scored a parent's night feed, eaten over a bowl of cereal, as sleep ([Whoop Community](https://www.community.whoop.com/t/wildly-inaccurate-sleep/13845)). For phone-based sleep apps, a 2026 analysis of 1–3★ reviews calls alarm failure "the single most common complaint" ([unstar.app](https://unstar.app/blog/sleep-cycle-pillow-sleepscore-oura-calm-sleep-tracking-apps-ranked-2026)). Whoop's smart alarm fires only "in the green" ([Whoop Community](https://www.community.whoop.com/t/feature-request-sleep-stage-aware-smart-wake-alarm/14612)).

Framing is the second cluster. Rise's notification "You owe your body some sleep" upset users who were sleeping 7–8 hours ([App Store, RISE](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rise-sleep-tracker/id1453884781?see-all=reviews)). In AASM's 2025 survey, **48% of US adults used a sleep tracker and 51% of those users felt anxious about their score** ([AASM](https://aasm.org/sleep-tracking-and-sleepmaxxing-change-bedtime-behaviors-and-keep-some-americans-awake-at-night/)). Clinicians treat CBT-I sleep diaries, not device scores, as the tool for insomnia ([UMN CBT-I Coach pilot](https://experts.umn.edu/en/publications/a-randomized-controlled-pilot-study-of-cbt-i-coach-feasibility-ac/)). Users also want their data out: **Oura is closing its web dashboard, including CSV download and the two-metric correlation view** ([Gadgets & Wearables, 21 Jul 2026](https://gadgetsandwearables.com/2026/07/21/oura-web-dashboard-shutdown-users-push-back/)). Shift workers and new parents are the clearest unserved groups. Legacy algorithms correctly identify only **50.3% of daytime sleep** in night-shift workers ([ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06670287](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06670287)). New mothers' longest uninterrupted stretch stayed around **3 hours in weeks 2–7** even after their total sleep recovered ([Powers Health](https://www.powershealth.org/about-us/newsroom/health-library/2025/06/06/interrupted-sleep-plagues-new-mothers)).

nütn has the same bookkeeping gap. `getSleepData` queries one fixed window from 18:00 yesterday to 12:00 today and adds every sample into a single total (`src/services/healthkit.js:L467-560`). That design means three things. A nap cannot be separated from the night. Day sleep after noon is lost. Overlapping samples from a watch and a ring or phone would likely be counted twice; this is an inference to test, because there is no source filter. A code comment also says the current HealthKit plugin "collapses every non-inBed sample" to "Asleep" (L504–506), so the stage breakdown that round 1's inventory recorded as HAVE is probably empty in the shipping build. On framing, nütn is mixed. Debt is shown in hours, as users ask, but REST passes only 7 nights to a 14-night engine and turns the number red above 2 hours (`src/components/RestModule.jsx:L783, L862`). Round 1's hide-numbers setting covers FEED calories and weight only (`src/utils/hideNumbers.js`). One part already works well: readiness stays null until the check-in is submitted, so the "rate how you feel first" pattern Whoop users request is half-built (`RestModule.jsx:L1044-1095`).

## Runners revolt over paywalls on their own data

The loudest cardio complaints of 2024–2026 are about pricing and trust. Strava moved Year in Sport, free since 2016, behind its roughly **$80/yr subscription in December 2025** ([road.cc](https://road.cc/content/news/strava-year-sport-now-only-subscribers-317425)). It made **API access require a paid subscription from 1 June 2026** ([pccentral.net](https://pccentral.net/strava-developer-program-paywall-paid-subscription-api-access/)). It also pulled training plans out of Premium after buying Runna ([Strava Community Hub](https://communityhub.strava.com/general-chat-2/strava-premium-is-no-longer-worth-it-after-runna-integration-10839)). Garmin Connect+ at $6.99/mo prompted an upvoted boycott call, "We need to take a firm stand" ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/health-fitness/smartwatches/furious-garmin-users-revolt-over-new-subscription-service-we-need-to-take-a-firm-stand)). Zwift raised its US price by 33% ([Gran Fondo Daily News](https://granfondodailynews.com/2024/05/08/zwift-announces-30-price-increase-users-revolt-and-cancel-memberships/)). Reliability comes next. In 596 low-star Strava reviews, **40 said an activity would not save and 38 said recording stopped mid-run** ([unstar.app](https://unstar.app/blog/strava-not-recording-activity-not-saved-gps-reviews-2026)). Recording one run in two apps duplicates it in Apple Health, and one guide calls that "the most common complaint" ([Sonar Health](https://www.sonarhealth.co/blog/sync-strava-to-apple-health/)). Apple users still cannot edit a saved workout ([Cult of Mac](https://www.cultofmac.com/how-to/how-to-add-delete-edit-apple-watch-workouts)), and treadmill distance reads "consistently 10-15% below the treadmill" with no way to fix it afterwards ([Apple Community](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/254912691)).

Hybrid athletes, who both lift and run, want one plan that keeps hard or long runs away from heavy leg days. No incumbent was found doing this. Runna treats strength as an add-on, Garmin's suggestions ignore lifting, and a crop of $4–19/mo startups now sells "fatigue transfer" as the feature ([Hypla](https://hypla.fit/); [HybridX](https://www.hybridx.app/)). Beginners quit at the Couch-to-5K week-5 jump to a 20-minute run. A cohort of 110 had **27.3% completion**, a figure that reached us only through blogs that sell alternative plans ([None to Run](https://www.nonetorun.com/blog/couch-to-5k-running-plan-alternative)). nütn already reads HealthKit workouts, but the merge removes duplicates only when date *and* source match, so two apps recording one run are both kept (`src/services/healthkitStore.js:L96-130`). Imported workouts appear in the calendar only on days without an IRON session (`src/components/WorkoutCalendar.jsx:L764-772`). The weekly and monthly reviews never read them (`src/engine/weeklyReview.js:L10-17`). The leg-day insight is literally a "Placeholder" (`src/engine/insights.js:L300-301`). Watch calorie error, measured at **27.96% MAPE** in a 2025 meta-analysis ([Univ. of Mississippi](https://olemiss.edu/news/2025/06/apple-watch-accuracy-study/index.html)), is already handled by round 1's eat-back and energy de-duplication, which is being built now.

## Habit, mood and coach apps lose people to guilt, billing traps and invented numbers

Habit users mostly ask for honest pause states. The Loop Habit Tracker issue asking to skip a day "without breaking the streak" ran for four years before skip support was announced, and one commenter wanted to "not lie about completing the habit" ([GitHub uhabits #88](https://github.com/iSoron/uhabits/discussions/88)). A 2026 roundup quotes a user who **lost a 200-day meditation streak to a hospital stay** ([unstar.app](https://unstar.app/blog/mental-health-app-reviews-what-users-say-about-wellbeing-apps-2026)). Calm's streak widget "only serves to raise my anxiety" ([Calm App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/calm/id571800810?see-all=reviews&platform=mac)). Billing anger dominates meditation reviews, from Headspace charging after cancellation ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/headspace.com)) to Fabulous trial charges in a thread titled "Predatory scam app developer" ([Apple Community](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/256002729)). Notification controls are the next complaint. Fabulous users cannot silence the non-reminder pings without silencing everything ([Trustpilot IE](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/thefabulous.co?page=7)). In WeightWatchers' December 2025 redesign, **30.3% of 178 negative reviews were about billing and 13.5% described sync that "fails silently"** ([unstar.app](https://unstar.app/blog/weightwatchers-app-what-it-really-costs-reviews-2026)). Journal users want local-only storage and a lock separate from the device passcode ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=20590555); [BGR](https://www.bgr.com/tech/iphone-journal-app-is-now-available-but-theres-something-you-should-do-before-you-use-it/)).

AI coaches fail in two ways. Whoop's coach admitted it had **made up a user's elevation gain** and invented night wakings, and the Reddit thread was titled "Whoop AI is legit slop" ([Gear Patrol](https://www.gearpatrol.com/fitness/whoop-ai-reddit-user-feedback-pros-cons/)). Fitbit's Gemini coach asked a long-time dog walker whether they could "ditch the dog to speed up" ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/fitbits-gemini-ai-coach-is-giving-users-unhinged-fitness-advice-heres-why-users-are-saying-they-cannot-wait-for-my-trial-to-end)). Strava's AI praised the "consistent, well-rounded" fitness of a cyclist who had just been hit by a car ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2024/10/11/strava-app-artificial-intelligence-fitness-athletic-memes)). The market responded on **8 May 2026 with Whoop's "My Memory"**, an editable list of goals, injuries and temporary states, launched alongside proactive check-ins ([WHOOP Locker](https://www.whoop.com/us/en/thelocker/my-memory-whoop/)). What people pay human coaches $149–199/mo for is mostly someone who notices: "My coach messages me almost every day" ([Garage Gym Reviews](https://www.garagegymreviews.com/best-personal-training-apps)). nütn's strongest asset here is server-side. The coach persona must "only cite numbers that appear in the health context", and a lint rejects any reply in which "EVERY NUMERAL… MUST APPEAR IN THE EVIDENCE" is not met (`backend/services/coach_service.py:L35-38`; `backend/coach_core/lint.py:L1-30`). That is the safeguard Whoop lacked, although the chat still depends on round 1's backend hardening. The gaps are on the client. The streak engine has automatic freezes and grace days but no pause the user can set. Sick, Vacation and Travel modifiers exist in `src/engine/tdee.js:L458-500`, but no screen uses them. The v7 "Notifications · REMINDERS · QUIET HOURS" row opens general settings, which have no notification toggles (`src/components/v7/screens/You.jsx:L146-151`). A second mood check-in replaces the first for the day (`src/components/mind/MindModule.jsx:L273`). There is no journal lock.

## Weight, fasting, hydration and meal-planning apps fail on data custody

Three meal planners have closed in under two years: **Yummly (December 2024), PlateJoy (1 July 2025) and Mealime (21 October 2026)**, none with a usable bulk export ([PlateJoy support](https://support.platejoy.com/platejoy-faqs/35663); [Pann](https://www.pann-app.com/blog/is-mealime-shutting-down)). Yummly users "lost everything I saved", and one had over 600 recipes that could be exported only one PDF at a time ([unitq](https://unitq.com/unitq-scorecards/yummly)). Weight apps have the same custody problem. Happy Scale data was "gone with no way to retrieve it" after a phone upgrade ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id532430574?see-all=reviews)). Eufy's sync to Apple Health stopped silently, and the app required an account for a Bluetooth-only scale ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1153481724/eufylife/reviews)). Consumer smart scales miss **fat mass by a median 2.2–4.4 kg against DEXA** ([JMIR mHealth 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33929337/)). Fasting apps draw renewal-shock complaints such as "signed up for a week's trial for $9… they were charged $79" ([Trustpilot, Simple](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/simple-life-app.com)). They also sell measured-looking "zones", although "there is currently no method available to measure autophagy in humans" ([Consensus](https://consensus.app/home/blog/does-the-science-match-the-hype-on-intermittent-fasting/)). Hydration apps paywall undo: "no option to delete water that was added… unless you pay $8.99" ([App Store, Waterllama](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/water-tracker-waterllama/id1454778585?see-all=reviews)). Progress-photo apps paywall the photos themselves ([App Store, Progress](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/body-measurement-photo-weight-tracker-progress/id583840813)).

The code check gives nütn mixed marks here. On the good side, progress photos are free and unlimited, with side-by-side comparison and measurements (`src/components/ProgressPhotos.jsx:L20-210`). Water undo is free (`src/components/WaterWidget.jsx:L64-170`), and HealthKit weight merges into one body-metrics store with a source tag (`src/services/bodyMetrics.js:L1-20`). Against that, **the fasting timer ships exactly the claims critics call unmeasurable**: "Ketosis", "Autophagy", "Deep Autophagy" and "Immune Reset" stages (`src/components/FastingTimer.jsx:L30-57`; `src/engine/fasting.js:L33-52`). Logging any meal, even black coffee, ends the fast (`src/services/foodLogger.js:L43-48`). HealthKit weight history is fetched for 90 days only (`src/services/native.js:L99`). There is no meal planner or grocery list. On a cut, continued loss below goal still turns the trend green (`src/components/FeedModule.jsx:L1918-1935`), the wording one Happy Scale reviewer said rewards disordered habits ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/532430574/happy-scale/reviews)).

## Women, beginners, older, injured and disabled users get one-size-fits-all apps

Women's health moved fast in 2026. In July, Mozilla gave its only 10/10 to the period tracker that **"stores health information locally and requires no account"** (Euki), and Stardust scored 2 ([CyberInsider](https://cyberinsider.com/mozilla-study-ranks-euki-as-the-most-private-period-tracker/)). A jury found Meta liable for intercepting Flo users' data in August 2025 ([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/online-security/jury-finds-meta-illegally-collected-data-from-womens-health-app-flo-what-you-need-to-know)). Apple's iOS 27 adds free perimenopause support ([Femtech Insider](https://femtechinsider.com/apple-adds-menopause-and-perimenopause-support-to-health-app/)). Oura added hormonal birth-control support on 6 May 2026 ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/oura-launches-hormonal-health-features-3662688/)). Rolling-average predictions still fail anyone whose cycles swing from 25 to 35 days ([pcostracker.app](https://www.pcostracker.app/blog/flo-app-alternatives/)). Cycle-sync workout apps offer "low-impact workout format only" rather than progressive overload ([health-time](https://www.health-time.com/en/article/1145_1_cd_womens-health_story_guide_en_reviewed-cycle-diet-app-2026-minutes)), which leaves a strength-plus-cycle niche open. nütn names the module "Cycle" and offers it to anyone whose profile does not say male (`src/components/FeedModule.jsx:L1532-1533`), which is the neutral framing advocates ask for. But it predicts from averages only, has no irregular, perimenopause or postmenopause mode, and syncs `PERIOD_LOG` to the cloud along with everything else (`src/services/cloudSync.js:L53`).

For beginners, the first month matters most. In a 522,994-user cohort, consistency in the **first 28 days was the strongest predictor of long-term adherence**, and only 18.1% were still adherent at six months ([SportRxiv 709](https://sportrxiv.org/index.php/server/preprint/view/709)). A brand-commissioned poll found 51% of gym-goers had felt intimidated ([Musclebooster](https://musclebooster.welltech.com/gymtimidation-us)). Lifters with injuries get free-text notes in Strong ([Strong Help](https://help.strongapp.io/article/134-how-do-notes-work)), and Hevy offers injury handling only inside its paid Trainer ([Hevy](https://www.hevyapp.com/features/workout-plan-generator/)). nütn's version is worse than either, because it makes a promise it does not keep. The assessment collects injured areas and tells the user "Programs will avoid aggravating movements" (`src/engine/assessment.js:L77-88, L432`). Yet the filter that honours a blacklist and `disabledMuscles` has **no caller anywhere** (`src/engine/workoutProgramEngine.js:L719-760`), and the set row has no pain field. On accessibility, AppleVis users say workout apps "almost work, but they don't work with VoiceOver for adjusting reps or sets" ([AppleVis](https://www.applevis.com/forum/ios-ipados/free-accessible-app-tracking-workouts)). Trainerize users were still asking for Dynamic Type in February 2026 ([ABC Trainerize ideas](https://ideas.abcfitness.com/forums/167887-coach-trainer-abc-trainerize/suggestions/16372018-adjust-the-app-to-support-dynamic-type-so-that-tex)). Older adults cite "insufficient font size, lack of screen magnification" ([PMC12761059](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12761059)). nütn sets `body` to `-apple-system-body` (`src/styles/global.css:L2411-2475`), but components carry **2,377 inline numeric `fontSize` values** that ignore it, and the weight and rep steppers are 28×28 px, well under Apple's 44 pt minimum (`src/components/ds/WorkoutSetRow.jsx:L64-80`).

## Three openings close on fixed dates

**Mealime closes on 21 October 2026, and its users cannot export recipes, plans or grocery lists** ([MealThinker](https://mealthinker.com/blog/mealime-alternative); [Swoodie](https://swoodie.app/blog/mealime-shutting-down)). A free app that imports recipes from a pasted URL and promises "export anytime" can catch those users over the next 12 days, and nütn's backend recipe-URL import is already being built. **Strava's paywalled Year in Sport arrived in December 2025** ([Gadgets & Wearables](https://gadgetsandwearables.com/2025/12/20/strava-year-in-sport/)), and Garmin's recap is paywalled too. If the pattern repeats this December (an inference, not an announcement), a free yearly recap that combines cardio, lifting, food and sleep should ship by late November. **Oura's removal of web CSV and correlation charts** (reported July 2026) leaves data-minded ring users wanting a free export and a pick-two-metrics chart. Two other dates change what is worth building. Apple's free perimenopause tracking in iOS 27 means nütn should read cycle data from HealthKit and link it to training, not compete as a standalone period tracker. Whoop's My Memory (May 2026) has made an editable coach-memory screen table stakes for any LLM coach.

## Round 2 corrects four round-1 assumptions

First, **round 1's R7 said Whoop's cycle coaching excludes hormonal-contraception users. That is outdated.** Whoop's March 2026 white paper says its model "adapts for variable cycles, perimenopause, hormonal birth control" ([Whoop](https://www.whoop.com/us/en/thelocker/menstrual-cycle-insights-white-paper/); [Wareable](https://www.wareable.com/health-and-wellbeing/whoop-women-blood-biomarker-panel-advanced-labs-hormonal-symptom-predictions)), and Oura followed in May. A contraception-aware path is therefore table stakes, not a differentiator. That makes irregular, perimenopause and postmenopause modes the new gap (U1 below). Second, round 1 used Oura Advisor's early "drink more water" answers as its example of generic AI. In 2024–2025 hands-on reviews Oura Advisor is praised as "not an intrusive or obnoxious tool", with a choice of supportive or direct tone ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/oura-advisor-hands-on-3472107/)). The live AI complaints are now invented data (Whoop) and context-blind optimisation (Fitbit). Third, round 1's inventory marked HealthKit sleep-stage import as HAVE. The code comment at `src/services/healthkit.js:L504-506` says the plugin collapses stages to "Asleep", and nights come from one fixed window, so treat it as PARTIAL. Fourth, round 1's numbers-off item (R3) was built for FEED only. The sleep evidence (51% score anxiety, orthosomnia) shows it needs to cover REST and readiness too (S4).

## The prioritized round-2 feature list

**How to read the tables.** IDs do not overlap round 1's F, W, C, N and R series. **P0** means a shipping defect, a false claim, or a loud, widespread complaint that blocks trust. **P1** means a strong complaint signal with high differentiation, or a cheap fix to a repeated complaint. **P2** means quieter demand, or a build that is expensive relative to its evidence. **HAVE** means it works in the build-next branch. **PARTIAL** means it exists but is limited, unmounted or covers only part of the complaint. **GAP** means absent. **BUILDING** means it is in the in-progress round-2 lanes. **⚑ owner** marks work that needs owner-only native changes (Info.plist, entitlements, `services/healthkit.js` permissions or plugin, `capacitor.config.ts`, widgets or Live Activities, or keys). Items already in round 1's list are excluded: eat-back and energy de-duplication (C7), weekly budget (C8), cycle-aware training (R7), readiness-to-nutrition (R8), HealthKit workout and nutrition writes (W8, N12), demo videos (W10) and forgot-to-finish (W7).

## Conclusion

Round 1 found that users leave loggers over trust failures. Round 2 shows the same pattern in every adjacent category, with one twist: **the most fixable complaints are about bookkeeping that HealthKit-reading software can fully own** — which sleep counts as the night, which run is the duplicate, which day was a sick day, and where the recipes go when the app dies. Incumbents keep getting these wrong because their business is the hardware, the subscription or the content library. nütn's business is the record, so being visibly correct about the record is the most credible thing it can sell. It also changes what "all-in-one" should mean in practice: not more tabs, but joins no single-purpose app can make. A meal timestamp can explain a bad night, a leg day can be kept clear of a long run, and a cycle phase can explain a heavy set.

The code check also produced a sharper warning than round 1. nütn's brand rests on "nothing fabricated", and the backend lint shows real discipline there. Yet the client still ships an injury promise it does not keep, fasting stages science cannot measure, and a sleep total that can double-count. Those are the same failures the research shows users punishing in Whoop, Fitbit and the fasting apps, so they belong at the top of the queue ahead of any new feature. Because every user quote here is secondhand and many sources are rivals, the next research step is unchanged: direct App Store and Reddit mining, once access opens, to turn these loudness judgements into counts. The one exception is the Mealime deadline, which does not need more evidence to act on.
